PART C: Assertion-Reason (A-R) Pairs
Options Guide:
A = Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
B = Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
C = A is true but R is false.
D = Both A and R are false (or A is false but R is true).
30. Assertion (A): In studying physiological and behavioural processes, one can take a physico-chemical approach using cell-free systems.
Reason (R): A physico-chemical approach enables us to describe various biological processes in molecular terms.
31. Assertion (A): The cell is the fundamental structural and functional unit of all living organisms.
Reason (R): Anything less than a complete structure of a cell does not ensure independent living.
32. Assertion (A): In the fluid mosaic model proposed by Singer and Nicolson, the fluidity of the membrane can be measured.
Reason (R): The quasi-fluid nature of lipid enables the lateral movement of proteins within the overall bilayer.
33. Assertion (A): The nuclear envelope forms a physical barrier between the materials present inside the nucleus and that of the cytoplasm.
Reason (R): The nuclear envelope consists of two parallel membranes with a perinuclear space measuring 10 to 50 nm between them.
34. Assertion (A): The nucleus plays a major role in heredity and controls the activities of cellular organelles.
Reason (R): The nucleus contains nucleoli and a chromatin network.
35. Assertion (A): Mitochondria, chloroplasts, and peroxisomes are not considered a part of the endomembrane system.
Reason (R): The functions of mitochondria, chloroplasts, and peroxisomes are highly coordinated with the endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi complex.
